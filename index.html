Ring 1 of 4 · Philosophy

Who we are. Who we serve. How we work together.

One philosophy. One network. One coordinated team around every business owner and their family.

Our philosophy

Who we serve

Canadian business owners and their families. Unapologetically. They are the backbone of this country and the least-supported clients in financial services.

Why we serve them

Their lives are complex, their advisors are siloed, and the resulting fragmentation costs them money, time, and peace of mind. We exist to fix that.

How we serve them

As one coordinated team, working alongside the advisor the business owner trusts most, to make that advisor the one they value most. One table. One plan. Personal life, business life, and every advisor aligned around what matters most to the business owner and their family.

Two names, one way of working

The philosophy · the network

Integrated Advisory™

Our philosophy for serving business owners, and the network of member firms that practise it together, built to elevate every trusted advisor into their clients' most valued advisor.

The founding firm · est. 1999

WealthCo.

WealthCo has lived this philosophy since 1999. We formalised it into a network so every Canadian business owner could experience what wealth advisory should be.

How we make decisions

Every decision we make, in every firm and every department, is tested against four questions. If a decision cannot pass all four, we rework it until it can.

01

Does this serve a Canadian business owner and their family, directly or indirectly?

02

Does this make the advisor at the centre of the client's life more valued, not just more present?

03

Would a network member be proud to deliver this to a business owner they personally cared about?

04

Does the same thing serve the business owner, the network member, and WealthCo, without fragmentation between them?

Source: WealthCo Group Philosophy one-pager, v1.0

Ring 2 of 4 · Methodology

The walking of the talking.

Philosophy sets the standard. Methodology is how we walk the talk. The operations of the business, the advisory side, and how those come together. Processes that carry a client from first contact through ongoing care. People who coordinate across every discipline at the table. Data that keeps the whole system honest.

Ring 3 of 4 · Tools and technology

Facilitate. Serve. Make sense.

Tools earn their place by what they do for the rings around them. Three jobs, tested against the outcomes at the centre.

Job 1

Facilitate processes

Automate the repetitive. Speed the handoffs. Remove the friction between stages.

Job 2

Serve people

Give advisors and teams what they need, when they need it, so the client conversation is always the priority.

Job 3

Make sense of data

Turn information into decisions. What is stalled, what is urgent, what matters most right now.

Knowledge workers using AI complete 25% more tasks, 25% faster, at 40% higher quality.

Source: Harvard Business School / BCG, 2024

The centre · Outcomes

How do we better serve business owners?

Every ring points inward. The philosophy sets the standard, the processes and people and data execute it, the tools accelerate it. The outcome is always the same question answered better: how do we serve the Canadian business owner and their family?

Decisions used to wait for data

Now data waits for decisions. A plain-language query against the leadership dashboard returns a clean answer in seconds: active referrals, what is stalled, what is flagged urgent.

Source: WealthCo Annual Retreat, April 2026, "The operating principle" and "Tangible example 04"
